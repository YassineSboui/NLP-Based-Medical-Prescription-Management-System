LIMITATIONS
  - Every row for a label descends from one or two CDC/WHO passages, so folds are not clinically independent. The cross-source transfer diagnostic is the honest measure of generalisation; the test accuracy is an upper bound.
  - The external scenario set has 14 items. Its interval is correspondingly wide.
  - 14 labels only. Anything outside them is answered as the nearest of the 14, which is why the API ships a confidence floor and an abstention path.
